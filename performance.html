04 Method

Definitions & scope

How it's
measured.

Every number on this page comes from Instagram Insights for the reporting period — no estimates, no rounding up.

Role & scope. Creative Digital Marketing Supervisor at MKG Indonesia. All figures represent account-level Instagram performance during the reporting period.

Source. Instagram Insights for @mkg_indonesia (MKG INDONESIA), 20 Sep–19 Nov 2026 (60 days).

Reach & views. 118,109 total views (+16.5%), 23,207 accounts reached (+80.4%), 1,996 followers, and 2,913 profile visits. Total views represent the full 60-day account overview, while the IPK table compares the three core content formats (Posts, Reels, and Stories).

Engagement rate. ER = Interactions ÷ Views. Posts 1.37%, Reels 2.78%, Stories 0.14%.

Content Performance Index. IPK = 50% × ER (normalized) + 50% × Views (normalized), on a 0–100 scale. Each format's views and ER are normalized against the highest-performing format in the comparison before combining. Top by IPK: Reels 87.9 · Posts 74.6 · Stories 27.3.
